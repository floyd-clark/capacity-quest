## Additional Complexity Lab
Fourteen authored quantitative problems (two/domain) use three traversed stations and six decisions: investigation; governing calculation; bounded-scope/full-hold branch; technical/commercial/service decision rights; verification; consequence update. Wrong tests consume modeled diagnostic minutes; a missed 45-minute checkpoint becomes explicit in the authority/escalation commitment. These are modeled investigation costs, not a timer that punishes reading speed.

Both bounded acceptance and full hold can be valid. Full hold resolves the learning exercise with zero accepted new scope, no newly authorized recovery spend and a stated recovery estimate/uncertainty. Bounded acceptance is limited to the case’s qualified scope, obtains authority and completes its synthetic close test. The unresolved population stays held. Commercial spend approval cannot count conditional supply as accepted service. Probabilistic expected value does not create a deterministic recovery date. Monetary amounts are spend caps, not a claim of money already spent.

Debriefs and printable exports show the equation/result, accepted versus installed scope, held population, spend cap, uncertain recovery timing, next evidence checkpoint and operational/business consequence. Each case retains its source-case IDs. Hints do not reduce credit; wrong decisions do. Rubric grades constrained selections, not real engineering competence or speech semantics.

## Reference boundary
The source pack’s reference register is not treated as independent proof of every external specification. New advanced problems use explicitly fictional parameters rather than generation-specific TPU numbers or real site operating limits. Scenario close tests are scripted teaching outcomes, not executed electrical/thermal tests. All real electrical, coolant, interlock and safety work is reserved to qualified authorities and approved procedures.

Official Google documentation was consulted during this update for the general value of profiling, memory/shape behavior and workload bottlenecks: [Cloud TPU performance guide](https://docs.cloud.google.com/tpu/docs/performance-guide) and [Profile your model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/tpu/docs/profile-tpu-vm). These are optional reference links; the game never requests them and needs no network. No generation table or fixed cloud configuration recommendation was copied into the advanced simulations.
