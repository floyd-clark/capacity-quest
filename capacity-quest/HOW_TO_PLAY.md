# Operator quick start
1. Select How to Play for framework definitions, then Start tutorial stage.
2. Move right with D, jump with Space, and collect cyan evidence chips. Chips reward exploration; they cannot substitute for diagnostic evidence.
3. Stand near a blue station and press E. Read the observation and identify the best discriminating test. The simulation reveals a labeled synthetic evidence result after that choice.
4. Choose bounded containment, assign accountability and real decision authority, and require the scenario's close test. Wrong choices remain recoverable; they never unlock service.
5. Repeat at the next two stations. Checkpoint activates after the second. Avoid red patrols; amber risk barriers disappear after containment. R retries from the checkpoint.
6. Reach the service door; on a boss stage, reject local-green shortcuts and obtain integrated acceptance. Continue or export your debrief.

Friday 60-minute practice route: Fabric Skyway 20m; Rackworks Factory 15m; Commissioning Kingdom 15m; Interview Sprint 10m. Use World Select and focus on evidence, rollback/hold options, acceptance gates, named owner and verification. The route is a suggested time budget, not a measured game completion time.

## Complexity Lab
Choose menu 09. Traverse the three stations in order. At each station complete two decisions. Calculate before you commit; both a qualified bounded scope and an explicit full hold are defensible. Do not report installed inventory or forecast supply as accepted service. After the final station, reach the REVIEW exit and export your quantified decision memo. Menu 10 filters the 224 source cases by domain and lifecycle.
