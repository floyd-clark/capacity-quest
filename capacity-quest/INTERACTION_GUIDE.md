# Clickable pictures and hands-on missions

Choose the featured **Hands-on missions** picture or menu 09. Click a mission thumbnail to enter its platform stage. The three field stations each contain two activities:

1. **Inspect and compare.** Click numbered parts of the infrastructure picture to read evidence and pin two probes. Run a comparison across the boundary that distinguishes the competing explanations. The objective and hints explain what to compare; arbitrary clicks cannot grant acceptance.
2. **Calculate.** Enter the governing quantity in the stated units. The equation is available as a reference. The game parses a number; it never evaluates entered code or equations.
3. **Quarantine.** Toggle qualified and unresolved populations between proposed service and HOLD. Unresolved units cannot be silently included in a release. Moving everything to HOLD is a defensible branch with zero new accepted scope.
4. **Connect decision rights.** Select or drag a role onto the decision it owns. Technical evidence, service/customer scope and capped spending have different signatories.
5. **Sequence recovery.** Drag the work cards into order or use their labeled earlier/later buttons. Exposure is bounded before change, and representative evidence precedes final acceptance. This is a simulated decision sequence, not instructions to manipulate live electrical or cooling systems.
6. **Compose an update.** Enter accepted and held counts, include the accountable owner/next evidence/stop commitment, and optionally write a one-sentence recommendation. The counts and commitment are checked; the free-text recommendation is ungraded and escaped in printable exports.

Tab and Enter operate the buttons. Text/number fields and checkboxes use standard keyboard controls. Roles can be clicked instead of dragged; sequence cards have up/down buttons. H gives a reasoning hint. Settings can turn **Hands-on lab activities** off for classic answer cards, and the return-to-field button preserves the current session.

Campaign and Deep Library stations also contain clickable domain pictures. Those inspections reveal contextual evidence and preserve the original diagnostic gates; inspecting a picture alone never certifies readiness.

All seven SVG pictures are original, locally shipped assets. No image service or network connection is required to use them. For offline play keep the entire assets folder with the JavaScript, CSS and HTML.
